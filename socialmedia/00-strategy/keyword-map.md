# UniHamper — Keyword Map (SEO)

> Obeys `brand-source-of-truth.md`. Keywords are grouped by **audience × intent**.
> **Priority** is a practical call for a brand-new domain (P1 = do first, P3 = later/after authority builds).
> **Intent tags:** `INFO` (informational/top-funnel) · `COMM` (commercial/comparison) · `BOFU` (bottom-of-funnel, ready to act).

---

## How to read priority (context for a 0-authority domain)
- **P1** = low competition + high intent OR local terms once campuses are set. Fastest ranking + best conversion. Start here.
- **P2** = medium competition, strong relevance. Build once a few P1 pages exist to link from.
- **P3** = high competition / broad head terms (e.g. "laundry service"). Long game; needs domain authority first.

Local + long-tail beats head terms every time at launch. We win the specific, not the generic.
