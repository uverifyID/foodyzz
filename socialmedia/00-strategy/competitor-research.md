## 1. The competitive landscape (three buckets)

Laundry-delivery competitors fall into three structurally different models. UniHamper only truly
overlaps with one of them (marketplaces), and beats it on trust + economics.

| Bucket | Who | What they actually are |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Gig marketplaces** | SudShare | App matches customers to *individual freelancers* washing in their own homes |
| **B. Owned-ops / vertically integrated** | Rinse, Laundryheap, Hamperapp | The company runs the fleet and/or the facilities; you're their customer, not a laundromat's |
| **C. Single-operator campus shops** | Tide Cleaners (campus), WeWash, Campus Wash & Fold, Smart Sudz | One laundromat/brand serving a fixed set of campuses at *their* fixed prices |

UniHamper is a **marketplace of real local laundromats** — the trust of bucket C, the scale/asset-light
model of bucket A, with $0 commission and campus-native design none of them combine.

## 2. Competitor teardown

### SudShare — the closest analog ("Uber for laundry")
- **Model:** Gig marketplace. Customers order pickup; independent "Sudsters" wash laundry **in their own homes**. ([Consumer Startups](https://www.consumerstartups.com/p/sudshare), [SideHusl](https://sidehusl.com/sudshare/))
- **Pricing:** $1/lb next-day, $2/lb same-day; $20 min (2-day) / $40 min (1-day). ([The Work at Home Wife](https://theworkathomewife.com/how-much-does-sudshare-pay/))
- **Commission:** **SudShare takes ~25%** — Sudsters keep 75¢/lb ($1.50 rush). ([The Work at Home Wife](https://theworkathomewife.com/how-much-does-sudshare-pay/))
- **Campus focus:** No — general consumer, home-washers.
- **Weaknesses:** Random individuals washing in home machines (trust/hygiene concern); 25% cut squeezes washers; no campus logistics (dorm addresses, RA drop points); no insured commercial facilities.
- **🎯 GAP UniHamper exploits:** Same "Uber for laundry" convenience, but **real insured laundromats** instead of strangers' basements, and **$0 platform commission** instead of 25%. *"Not a stranger's washing machine — a real, insured laundromat."*

### Tide Cleaners — Campus (the incumbent brand)
- **Model:** P&G-backed franchise/corporate service. Weekly on-campus **drop-off + pickup** at fixed campus locations; app tracks the laundry truck. ([Tide Cleaners Campus](https://tidecleaners.com/en-us/touchpoints/campus-laundry), [My Tide Cleaners](https://my.tidecleaners.com/campus))
- **Pricing:** **Subscription tiers** — 10 / 20 / 30 lb per week, billed monthly, per-semester, or annually; pay-per-order option exists. Prices set by Tide. ([Baylor plans](https://my.tidecleaners.com/campus/bay/subscriptions))
- **Campus focus:** Yes — strong, but limited to Tide's contracted campuses.
- **Commission model:** N/A — it *is* the operator; no local laundromats keep a rate.
- **Weaknesses:** Rigid lb-tier subscriptions (pay whether you use it or not); drop-off at set locations, not true door pickup on the student's schedule; corporate feel; a student can't earn from it; local shops are locked out entirely.
- **🎯 GAP UniHamper exploits:** No forced subscription — **pay only when your wash starts**, door pickup on *your* schedule, and it channels money to **local laundromats**, not a national brand. *"Skip the lb-plan you never finish. Pay $0 until your wash starts."*

### Rinse — premium urban owned-ops
- **Model:** Company-run "Valet" fleet + facilities; evening pickup 7–10pm. ([Rinse services & pricing](https://www.rinse.com/services-pricing/))
- **Pricing:** Flat-bag — **$45 Flex (~15 lb) / $75 Full (30+ lb)**, plus **$14.95 pickup/delivery**; $9.95 next-day rush; "Rinse Go" subscription $14.95/mo waives fees. ([Rinse cost FAQ](https://www.rinse.com/faqs/billing-and-payments/how-much-does-rinse-cost/))
- **Campus focus:** No — ~10 major metros. ([Rinse service areas](https://www.rinse.com/faqs/our-services/what-areas-do-you-serve/))
- **Commission model:** N/A — owned ops.
- **Weaknesses:** Expensive ($14.95 delivery + premium bag prices); big-city only, no campus presence; no local-shop economy; no earn-side for students.
- **🎯 GAP UniHamper exploits:** **$9.99 flat** delivery vs their $14.95, no subscription required, and we go where Rinse won't — campus towns. *"$9.99 flat. No $15 delivery fee, no big-city-only waitlist."*

### Laundryheap — aggregator using partner facilities
- **Model:** App books pickup; routes to **local partner facilities**; 24h delivery. ([How Laundryheap works](https://help.laundryheap.com/en/articles/6265329-how-does-laundryheap-work))
- **Pricing:** Wash & fold from ~$2.33/lb (varies by city) + service fee from **$4.95**; charged after the facility weighs it. ([Laundryheap pricing](https://www.laundryheap.com/en-us/pricing))
- **Campus focus:** No — metro / international.
- **Commission model:** Takes a margin off partner facilities (partners don't set the customer price; Laundryheap does).
- **Weaknesses:** Partners are price-takers, not price-setters; opaque final charge (weighed later); no campus logistics; no ambassador/earn side.
- **🎯 GAP UniHamper exploits:** We use local pros like they do, but **the pro sets the price and keeps 100%** — no platform cut. *"The laundromat sets the price and keeps every cent. We don't take a piece."*

### Hamperapp — on-demand, own service
- **Model:** On-demand pickup/clean/return within 48h, 7 days/week. ([Hamperapp](https://www.hamperapp.com/))
- **Pricing:** ~$1.50–$2.00/lb, **free pickup & delivery**, priced like local shops. ([Hamperapp wash & fold](https://www.hamperapp.com/services/wash-and-fold-laundry-services))
- **Campus focus:** No — metro (Miami, etc.).
- **Weaknesses:** No campus design; no marketplace of choosable local shops; no earn-side; limited geography.
- **🎯 GAP UniHamper exploits:** Campus-native from day one, plus a **student earn engine (ambassadors)** they simply don't have. *"Built for dorm life, not just doorsteps."*

### WeWash Laundry Co. — single-operator campus service
- **Model:** One operator, own facility; dorm/office door pickup, 24–48h. ([WeWash college plans](https://www.wewashlaundryco.com/pricing/college-service-48-hour-turn-around/))
- **Pricing:** **$2.30–$2.40/lb** (discount for recurring), set by WeWash. ([WeWash Drake](https://www.wewashlaundryco.com/service-areas/drake-university-laundry-service/))
- **Campus focus:** Yes — but tiny geography (a few Iowa campuses: Drake, Grandview, DMACC).
- **Weaknesses:** Single operator = single point of failure, capacity-capped, one price, one region; local competitor shops can't join.
- **🎯 GAP UniHamper exploits:** A **network of many local laundromats** competing on price/quality vs one shop's one rate. *"Not one shop's price — pick from the pros near your dorm."*

### Campus Wash & Fold — subscription, DC metro
- **Model:** Weekly wash/dry/fold; **uses on-campus Campus Ambassadors as drop points**; own dedicated machines. 12 DC-area campuses. ([Campus Wash and Fold](https://www.campuswashandfold.com/))
- **Pricing:** **Semester plan $575 / 16 weeks (~$36/wk)** — a big upfront commit. ([Campus Wash and Fold](https://www.campuswashandfold.com/))
- **Campus focus:** Yes — DC metro only.
- **Weaknesses:** Ambassadors are unpaid *drop-off points*, not commissioned recruiters who earn; heavy semester prepay; single operator; one region.
- **🎯 GAP UniHamper exploits:** Our ambassadors **earn 30% of UniHamper's commission on every order from their campus** (they don't just hold your bag), and there's **no $575 semester lock-in** — pay per order. *"Our campus reps get paid. And there's no $575 semester bill to swallow."* ⚠️ CONFIRM: keep any hard $ figure until confirmed (commission itself is confirmed — never say "30% of the order").

### Smart Sudz — local laundromat with delivery
- **Model:** One NJ laundromat offering residential/campus/commercial pickup & delivery, 24h. ([Smart Sudz](https://www.smartsudz.com/pick-up-and-delivery/))
- **Pricing:** Own shop, own rate; Essex/Hudson/Union County NJ only. ([Smart Sudz service areas](https://www.smartsudz.com/service-areas/))
- **Campus focus:** Partial — one of several verticals, not campus-native.
- **Weaknesses:** Represents the *exact shop UniHamper recruits* — a great local laundromat with no tech reach beyond its county. It's a partner profile, not a rival platform.
- **🎯 GAP / OPPORTUNITY:** Shops like Smart Sudz are **UniHamper's supply**. Positioning: give them app reach, tracking, and payouts for **$0 commission**. *"You already wash and fold. We just bring you the students — and take $0."*

## 3. Positioning wedge — the 4 angles where UniHamper wins

Each angle = one differentiator from the source-of-truth, mapped against who it beats, with an
exact messaging line to reuse across captions, blogs, and scripts.

### Wedge 1 — "Real laundromats, not a stranger's basement" (trust)
- **Beats:** SudShare (home washers), and reassures vs any faceless app.
- **Line:** **"Your clothes get washed by a real, insured laundromat — not a stranger's home machine."**

### Wedge 2 — "$0 commission, they set the price" (economics / provider love)
- **Beats:** SudShare (25% cut), Laundryheap (margin off partners), Tide/Rinse (fixed corporate price).
- **Line for shops:** **"You set your price. You keep 100%. UniHamper takes $0."**
- **Line for students:** **"No middleman markup — you pay the pro's real price plus $9.99 to bring it home."**

### Wedge 3 — "Campus-native, no subscription trap" (fit + flexibility)
- **Beats:** Tide (lb-tier subscriptions), Campus Wash & Fold ($575 semester), Rinse/Laundryheap (not on campus).
- **Line:** **"Built for dorm life. No lb-plan you never finish — pay $0 until your wash starts, $9.99 flat to your door."**

### Wedge 4 — "Get paid, don't just pay" (the ambassador earn engine)
- **Beats:** Everyone — no competitor lets students *earn* by recruiting students + laundromats.
- **Line:** **"Everyone else charges students. UniHamper pays them — become a campus ambassador and earn."** Commission is confirmed — 30% of UniHamper's commission per campus order (never "30% of the order"). ⚠️ CONFIRM ambassador signup URL before adding a CTA link, and keep any hard $ figure until confirmed.

## 4. Content gaps — SEO topics competitors ignore

Competitor sites are thin, transactional landing pages. They rank for "laundry service near me"
but ignore the *student-intent* and *shop-owner-intent* long tail. These are ours to own:

**Student-intent gaps (nobody owns the "how" content):**
- "How to do laundry in college / dorm laundry survival guide" — high search, competitors sell instead of teach.
- "Is a laundry service worth it for college students?" — comparison intent, no honest breakdown exists.
- "Wash & fold vs. doing it yourself: the real time + cost math" — cite loads/week + $9–12/wk data ([KU via Caldwell & Gregory](https://www.caldwellandgregory.com/blog/what-to-know-about-college-students-and-laundry/)). ⚠️ CONFIRM: never present any time-saved figure as a UniHamper stat — attribute to the cited source.
- "How to get laundry off your plate during finals / move-in week" — seasonal, high-intent, unclaimed.
- "{Campus} laundry pickup: dorm-by-dorm guide" — pure local SEO, zero competition once campus list is set. ⚠️ CONFIRM: launch campus list.

**Ambassador-intent gaps (essentially unclaimed nationally):**
- "How to make money on campus without a 9–5" / "campus jobs that fit a class schedule."
- "What is a campus ambassador and how much can you earn?" — UniHamper can own this category term.
- "Best side hustles for college students 2026" — broad, and we have a real product answer.

**Laundromat-owner-intent gaps (B2B long tail no consumer app targets):**
- "How laundromats can add pickup & delivery without hiring drivers."
- "Get more wash & fold customers near a college campus."
- "Laundry pickup app for laundromats with no commission" — a term SudShare/Laundryheap can't credibly rank for.

**Category-defining gap:** No competitor owns **"Uber for laundry"** as an educational hub. Build a
pillar page around it and internally link every student/ambassador/shop cluster to it.
