## 1. Content pillars (6)

Balanced across all three audiences from the SoT. Rough target mix in `()`.

| # | Pillar | Audience | What it does | Sample formats |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P1** | **"Never do laundry again"** — the core promise | Students | Show the magic: pickup → tracked → folded → delivered. Convert. | Reel demo, before/after, day-in-the-life |

| **P3** | **"Get paid, don't just pay"** — ambassadors | Ambassadors | Recruit the growth engine; show earning is real. | Talking-head, "day of a campus rep", testimonial |

**Mix guidance:** ~40% students (P1+P2), ~20% ambassadors (P3), ~15% providers (P4), ~15% trust (P5), ~10% brand/community (P6). Pre-launch, weight P6 (hype) + P3/P4 (build supply and ambassador pipeline *before* go-live so there's inventory on day one).

## 2. Weekly cadence per platform (realistic for a solo, brand-new account)

Reels/TikTok are the only real discovery engine at 0 followers, so **video is the priority**; static/carousels are the support layer. One filming session per week feeds everything.

| Platform | Posts/week | Format mix | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TikTok** @unihamper | **3** | 3 short vertical videos | Primary reach engine. Native, trend-aware, unpolished-OK. |
| **Instagram** @unihamperhq | **4** (feed) + Stories daily-ish | 2 Reels (repurpose TikToks) · 1 carousel · 1 static/meme; 3-5 Story frames/wk | Reels = the same videos as TikTok. Stories carry polls/countdown/UGC. |
| **Facebook** (page) | **2-3** | Repurpose 1-2 Reels + 1 link/announcement post | Mostly cross-post; older audience + local group shareability. Lowest effort. |

**Total original assets to produce per week: ~3 videos + 1-2 statics/carousels.** Everything else is
repurposing. This is the sustainable solo floor — do not exceed it pre-launch or it won't hold.

**Batch rule:** film all 3 videos in one session, design statics in one Canva sitting, then schedule the
week (Blotato once wired). Solo-friendly = batch + schedule, never post live daily.

## 4. Repeatable weekly template (use every week once the launch window ends)

Copy this block each week; fill the `___` and rotate pillars so no audience is neglected 2 weeks running.

```
WEEK OF: ______  ·  Theme: ______  ·  Seasonal hook: ______

PRODUCE (one batch session):
[ ] Video 1 — [P1 core promise] ______  → TikTok + IG Reel (+ FB)
[ ] Video 2 — [P2 value/dorm]   ______  → TikTok + IG Reel
[ ] Video 3 — [rotate P3 ambassador / P4 provider / P5 trust] ______ → TikTok + IG Reel
[ ] Static/Carousel 1 — [rotate P4/P5] ______ → IG feed (+ FB)
[ ] Meme/static 2 (optional) — [P2/P6] ______ → IG feed

SCHEDULE:
  TikTok: 3 videos (spread Mon/Wed/Fri-ish)
  IG:     2 Reels + 1 carousel + 1 static ; Stories 3-5 frames (poll, UGC, BTS, CTA)
  FB:     2-3 repurposed posts + any announcement

WEEKLY CHECKS:
[ ] Every post passes brand-source-of-truth (no invented facts/prices/campuses/stats)
[ ] No specific wash $/lb quoted — "prices set by your local pro"
[ ] Each audience (student / ambassador / provider) touched at least once
[ ] Any external stat is cited; ambassador commission uses the confirmed "30% of UniHamper's commission" phrasing (no hard $); any campus / signup URL is CONFIRMED
[ ] At least 1 clear real CTA present (Request a pickup / List your laundromat / Become an ambassador)
[ ] Reply to comments + reshare UGC within 24h (the only "live" daily task)
```

**The one sustainable rule for a solo operator:** batch-produce once, repurpose everywhere,
schedule the whole week, then the only daily job is *engaging* (comments/DMs/Stories) — never creating.
