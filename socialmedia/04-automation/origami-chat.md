# origami.chat — Auto-Finding Laundromats to Recruit

**What it actually is:** origami.chat is an **AI B2B prospecting tool**. You describe your ideal
customer in plain English and it uses live web-crawling agents (Google Maps, Yelp, state license
registries, LinkedIn, 15+ sources) to return a **qualified lead list with verified email/phone**.
It's strong exactly where UniHamper needs supply: local, offline businesses (it reportedly finds
2–3× more than Apollo/ZoomInfo in verticals like home services).

Site: https://origami.chat/

## The right job for it: SUPPLY + campus targeting (confirmed by Raj)
Raj has confirmed origami.chat will drive **two things**:
1. **The launch campus list** — decide/prioritize which campuses to target first (by market size,
   nearby laundromat density, student population). This unblocks all local SEO + geo content.
2. **The laundromat lead list per campus** — the sales-lead's recruitment pipeline.

Origami is a **supply-side + targeting tool**, not a customer-marketing tool. (Customer demand comes
from the social + SEO engines.) It can also surface **campus orgs / RA networks** to seed ambassadors.

## How to use it (laundromat recruitment)
1. **Prompt it per campus** (once Raj confirms the campus list). Examples:
   - "Laundromats and wash-and-fold/dry-cleaning businesses within 5 miles of `{campus}`, with a
     phone number and owner or manager contact."
   - "Independent laundromats near `{campus city, state}` that offer pickup or delivery."
2. **Export the enriched list** (name, address, phone, email, owner if available).
3. Feed it to the **sales-lead** playbook: `05-sales/laundromat-acquisition-playbook.md` (cold email
   variations, call script, 4-touch follow-up). Track in the "first 5 shops per campus" sheet.
4. Repeat per campus as UniHamper expands.
