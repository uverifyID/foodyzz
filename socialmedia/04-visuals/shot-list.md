# Photo & Video Shoot — Shot List

> **This is a launch blocker.** There are zero marketing-usable photographs in the repo. The only real bike image exists baked inside an app-store screenshot and served at runtime from Firebase Storage. Every post in the caption library needs an image.
>
> **Budget: half a day. One bike, one rider, one photographer, Manhattan.** This is the highest-leverage four hours in the entire launch plan.

---

## Before the shoot

- [ ] Pull the existing bike photography out of Firebase Storage into a shared folder — it may cover some studio needs
- [ ] Clean the demo bike. Every scuff shows at macro.
- [ ] Confirm the **UL 2849 mark on the frame** and **UL 2271 mark on the battery** are physically present and legible. If they are not, stop — that is a much bigger problem than a photo shoot (see `../OPEN-QUESTIONS.md` Q1).
- [ ] Book a rider. **Pay them.** A real delivery rider, ideally a Spanish or French speaker, ideally someone who'd also do a testimonial later.
- [ ] Helmet. Non-negotiable, in every frame with a person on the bike.
- [ ] Model release signed before anyone is photographed.
- [ ] Charge the battery — you'll want the display lit.

**Locations, in order:** a quiet block for the hero and detail work → outside a busy restaurant at dinner rush → the Deliverista Hub at 249 Broadway → a residential doorway for the delivery sequence.

**Timing:** start 2 hours before sunset. Golden hour for the street work, then the last 30 minutes for the lit-display and dusk shots.

---

## Absolute rules

1. **Helmet in every frame with a rider.** No exceptions, no "just this one."
2. **No riding above a plausible 15 mph**, no lane-weaving, no red lights run, no sidewalk riding.
3. **No stock photography. No AI-generated bikes or people.** It gets identified instantly and it costs the entire trust position — which is the one thing this brand actually has.
4. **Real riders, paid, with a signed release.**
5. **Never photograph a real customer's address, door number or licence plate.**
6. Shoot vertical first. Social is the primary consumer of everything here.
