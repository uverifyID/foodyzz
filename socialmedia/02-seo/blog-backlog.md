## Non-negotiables

1. **Date-stamp everything.** Freshness is the entire competitive advantage in this category.
2. **Link primary sources** — City Record, nyc.gov, FDNY, DCWP, NY Senate. The incumbents don't, and it's why they can be beaten.
3. **Never publish a pending bill as law.**
4. **One CTA per post, contextual.**
5. **Sixth-grade reading level.** Most readers are reading in a second language.
6. **Set update reminders** on posts 1, 5, 7 and 9 — they all decay with news.
7. **Every post gets an image** with real alt text describing the image, not stuffed with keywords.
