The estimation method is documented in [../00-research/keyword-research-english.md](../00-research/keyword-research-english.md). **The SERP-composition observations are real and are the far more reliable signal** — a page 1 full of out-of-town DTC brands or a German eBay listing tells you more than any volume number.

## The strategic read

Three things shape this map:

1. **Transactional keywords are tiny and contested.** "ebike rental nyc" and friends are low-volume, and Whizz/Zoomo already own them with real domain authority. Chasing them head-on is a losing first move.
2. **Informational keywords about NYC e-bike law are large, spiking, and defended by nobody credible.** Page 1 for "ebike laws nyc 2026" is entirely national DTC e-bike brands writing about a city they do not operate in. **Zero nyc.gov results.** That is the opening.
3. **The regulatory news cycle is doing our keyword research for us.** The 15 mph cap (Oct 2025), the criminal-summons reversal (March 2026), and the 70% battery-fire increase (2026) each invalidated every article written before them. Freshness is a weapon a new site can actually wield.

**So: win on rules and risk, convert on price.** Every informational page carries a contextual path to the plans page.

## Cluster D — Spanish (P1)

Full analysis and dialect notes in [../00-research/language-strategy.md](../00-research/language-strategy.md). Three rules:

1. **Use "renta/rentar," not "alquiler."** Whizz standardised on the Spain register; NYC's riders are Mexican, Guatemalan and Ecuadorian.
2. **Every page needs a geo modifier.** Un-geo'd Spanish queries return Spain e-commerce and bring zero qualified traffic.
3. **Drop "sin buró de crédito"** — that is the *Mexican* credit bureau, a domestic-Mexico term. The US-market objection term is **"sin SSN" / "sin seguro social" / "sin crédito."**

## Cluster E — French (P1, and the surprise)

**46.5% of NYC delivery workers are French-primary** (West African — Guinea, Mali, Senegal, Mauritania), per NYSERDA's 2025 intercept survey of 331 workers at 43 locations. That is nearly double the Spanish share (25.1%). **Both Whizz and Zoomo already built French websites.** Neither posts French social content.

| # | Keyword | Est. vol/mo | Difficulty | Intent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | location vélo électrique New York livraison | 30–80 | Med | Transactional |
| E2 | vélo électrique livreur New York | 20–60 | Med | Commercial |
| E3 | **louer vélo électrique NYC sans SSN** | 10–30 | **Low** ⭐ | Transactional |
| E4 | vélo électrique Uber Eats New York | 10–30 | Low–Med | Commercial |

**Caveat worth respecting:** many West African riders speak French as a *second* language alongside Wolof, Fula, Mandinka or Arabic, and literacy varies. French search volume likely under-indexes the 46.5% population share. Validate with a few hundred dollars of French vs. Spanish paid search before committing to a full French content build — but do the **social and field** work in French from day one regardless, because that does not depend on search behaviour.

## Cluster F — Do NOT build

| Language / topic | Why not |
|---|---|
| **Bengali SEO pages** | Bengali-script queries return English NYC results and Bangladesh-domestic retail. No competitor has `bn`. NYSERDA didn't include Bengali as a survey cohort. **Bangladeshi riders in NYC search in English.** Ceiling <20 visits/mo. Reach them physically instead — JBBA in Jackson Heights, mosques in Kensington, flyers, WhatsApp. One Bengali *trust page* linked from a flyer is a conversion asset, not a traffic asset. |
| **Chinese SEO pages** | 6.6% of the workforce and structurally off-Google. Zoomo's only Chinese page is a **HungryPanda partnership landing page** with no language switcher. Chinese-language NYC riders find shops by word of mouth via WhatsApp, and their information layer is **WeChat**, which Google cannot index. Ceiling <100 visits/mo. **Do the BD deal with HungryPanda instead**, get listed on nychinaren.com and usahuarenjie.com, pitch Documented's WeChat account, and work Flushing and Sunset Park physically. |
| **NYC third-party food delivery licence (DCWP, $200/2yr)** | Platform-side compliance topic. Couriers do not search it. nyc.gov and amlegal own it. Wrong audience entirely. |

## News-driven content windows, Aug–Dec 2026

Ranked by demand spike × ease of ranking. These are the moments where a small site can outrank a big one purely by being correct and current.

| Rank | Event | Status | Target keywords |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 ⭐⭐⭐ | **15 mph citywide cap**, effective Oct 24 2025; club lawsuit Feb 2026 ongoing | Live, litigated | A1, A2 |
| 2 ⭐⭐⭐ | **Battery fires +70% in 2026** (235 vs 138) while deaths fell to 1 | Live, escalating | A5, A6 |
| 3 ⭐⭐ | **Criminal summonses ended Mar 27 2026** — but NYPD wrote 257 anyway Mar 31–Jul 13 | Live, contradictory | A7 |
| 4 ⭐⭐ | **Minimum pay → $22.13** effective Apr 1 2026; next bump ~Apr 1 2027 | Annual, predictable | B3 |
| 5 ⭐⭐ | **DCWP tip enforcement** from Jan 26 2026; $550M alleged tip suppression | Live, high-emotion | B2, B3 |
| 6 ⭐ | **Moped point-of-sale registration**, effective Jan 7 2025; 27,000 seized in 2024 | Live | A3, A4 |
| 7 ⭐ | **PopWheels scale-up** — $4.7M, ~30 cabinets, $75/mo | Live, under-covered | B5 |

**Set a calendar reminder for Feb 1 and April 1 each year** — DCWP must announce the pay adjustment by Feb 1 and it takes effect Apr 1. That is a guaranteed annual traffic spike you can pre-write for.
