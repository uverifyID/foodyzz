## How to use this file
Each **pillar** (P1–P6 from the calendar) has a block below with: a one-line purpose, **hook formulas**,
and **fill-in caption templates** for **IG**, **FB**, and **TikTok**. Grab a hook, drop it on a template,
fill the brackets, add the matching hashtag set from the bottom. Voice = friendly, student-native, a little
cheeky, value-first, soft CTA. Short sentences. Skimmable.

**Platform quick-rules**
- **TikTok** = the caption is short; the *video/on-screen text* carries the message. Native, unpolished-OK. 3–6 hashtags.
- **IG** = caption can breathe (2–5 short lines + bullets). 8–15 hashtags, relevant not spam.
- **FB** = slightly fuller sentences, older + local-group audience, always drop the link (unihamper.com). 3–6 hashtags.

## Universal hook formulas (work across pillars)
Steal these openers; they earn the scroll-stop:
1. **POV:** — "POV: your laundry pile just became furniture."
2. **The honest question** — "Is a laundry service actually worth it? Let's do the math."
3. **Nobody tells you** — "Nobody tells freshmen this about dorm laundry."
4. **What's the catch** — "$0 commission? What's the catch? Fair question 👇"
5. **Two truths** — "Two ways to survive campus laundry. Only one gives you your weekend back."
6. **Countdown/tease** — "3 days until you never fold a shirt again."
7. **Relatable pain** — "It's 11pm, every dryer's taken, and you have class at 8."
8. **Numbers** — "$0 upfront. $9.99 flat. Folded and back at your door."

**Soft-CTA menu** (rotate, never hard-sell): "Link in bio." · "Request a pickup at unihamper.com." · "Follow so you don't miss launch." · "DM us '{keyword}'." · "Which one are you? 👇"
