## Rules for the calendar itself

1. **Never publish a blocked asset to fill a slot.** Move the next item up instead.
2. **A missed slot is fine. A wrong claim is not.**
3. **Reshuffle for news.** If something material happens — a new rule, a fire story, a pay announcement — drop the scheduled post and cover it same-day. Freshness is the whole SEO and social advantage.
4. **Answer every comment within 24 hours**, especially the hostile ones. A publicly answered price challenge is worth more than the post it's under.
5. **Log which field location produced which QR scan.** Kill the ones that don't convert by week 3.
