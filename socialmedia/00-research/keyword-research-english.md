# Foodyzz SEO Keyword Research — Clusters A & B

## ⚠️ Methodology & honesty disclaimer (read first)

I have **no access to Ahrefs, Semrush, Moz, or Google Keyword Planner** in this environment. Every volume figure below is an **estimate**, not a tool reading. I've used one consistent method and labeled confidence on each row:

**Estimation method ("NYC-scaling model"):**
1. Anchor on known US-wide demand for the head term (from general SEO priors — itself imprecise).
2. Apply a **NYC geo-modifier ratio of 3–8%** for gig-economy terms. NYC is ~2.5% of US population but ~8–12% of US app-delivery courier labor (~60,000+ workers per DCWP), so geo-modified gig queries over-index.
3. Cross-check against **SERP composition** — if page 1 is dominated by programmatic job-board pages (ZipRecruiter/Indeed), real volume exists; if it's dominated by generic non-NYC vendor blogs, the NYC-specific volume is thin and the keyword is *winnable but small*.
4. Cross-check against **news-event timing** — 2025/2026 regulatory changes create verified spikes.

**Volume is reported as a band, never a fake precise number.** Confidence: `MED` = anchored to a real SERP signal I observed; `LOW` = unverified estimate, treat as directional only.

## Competitive note: Whizz is your real SEO threat

`getwhizz.com/blog` runs **~228 articles** across 6 categories (How to Choose 58, For Delivery 55, FAQs 52, All About E-Bikes 33, Battery 18, Care & Maintenance 12). They already rank **#1 for "how to lock an electric bike"** and hold positions for UL 2849 and food-delivery insurance. They also publish head-to-head comparison content against **JOCO, Zoomo, and Arrow** — expect them to publish a Foodyzz comparison shortly after launch.

Two exploitable weaknesses:
1. **Their winning content is generic, not NYC-specific.** They rank on domain strength, not local authority. Genuinely local content (precinct data, actual cabinet locations, real ticket procedures) beats it.
2. **They're slow on regulatory freshness.** Nothing I saw addresses the March 2026 summons reversal or the 2026 fire surge.
