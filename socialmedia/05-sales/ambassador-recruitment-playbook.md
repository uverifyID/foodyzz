## 0. The offer in one breath (once numbers are confirmed)
> "UniHamper is a campus laundry marketplace launching Aug 1. As an ambassador, you're the campus plug — you
> get students using it AND sign up local laundromats, and you earn **30% of UniHamper's commission on every
> order from your campus**. It's flexible, it's remote-friendly, and it's real resume experience running growth for a startup."

### Why a student says yes (the honest levers)
1. **Earn while you're already on campus.** You earn **30% of UniHamper's commission on every order from your campus** — recruiting students and laundromats is how you drive those orders.
2. **Flexible.** Post, DM, table, or just tell friends — on your own schedule, around classes.
3. **Resume + reference.** "Campus growth lead / brand ambassador for a startup" is real, cite-able experience.
4. **Be the campus plug.** Early access, first to know, the person who put laundry-on-demand on the map here.
5. **Low commitment.** No quota to start; you do as much as you want.

### What we do NOT claim (guardrails)
- **Commission phrasing is fixed:** "30% of UniHamper's commission on every order from your campus." Never say "30% of every order" or "30% commission" (both imply 30% of order value — false). No hard per-order $ figure until Raj OKs it.
- No income promises ("make $X/month"). We don't have data and won't fake it.
- No signup URL until confirmed — say "link in bio / DM me" and swap in the real URL later.
- No fake scarcity ("only 2 spots left") unless a real cap exists and is confirmed.

## 1. Recruitment funnel — IG / TikTok DM cold-open → value → close

**Who to target:** students with real campus reach — RAs, RDs, orientation leaders, club presidents, athletes,
dorm-famous accounts, big group-chat admins. Reach > follower count.

### Step 1 — Cold open (short, human, no pitch yet)
> "Hey [Name]! I run @unihamperhq — we're launching campus laundry-on-demand this fall and building a small
> ambassador crew. You've got real reach on campus, so you came to mind. Mind if I share how it works?"

*(Wait for a yes before pitching. Permission-first keeps it non-spammy.)*

### Step 2 — Value (once they reply)
> "So UniHamper is basically 'Uber for laundry' — students request wash-and-fold, local laundromats handle it.
> As an ambassador you earn commission two ways: getting students to use it, and signing up local laundromats.
> It's flexible — you do it around classes — and it's genuine startup growth experience for your resume.
> You earn **30% of UniHamper's commission on every order from your campus** — happy to send the exact breakdown."

### Step 3 — Close (low-pressure, single next step)
> "If you want in, I'll send the ambassador signup + a starter kit (ready-made posts, DMs, and a laundromat
> pitch so you're not starting from scratch). Want me to send it over?"
>
> Then: **"Here you go — [⚠️ CONFIRM: signup URL]. Any questions, just reply here."**

**If no reply after ~4 days — one soft nudge, then stop:**
> "No worries if it's not your thing! If campus growth + earning ever sounds fun, I'm here. Either way, good
> luck this semester 🙌"

## 2. Email / DM for RAs & RDs (more formal, leans into their role)

RAs and RDs are ideal: trusted, know their residents, already solve dorm problems. Lead with fit + résumé value.

**Subject:** Paid campus ambassador role — good fit for an RA/RD?

Hi [Name],

I'm [Your name] with UniHamper, a campus laundry marketplace launching August 1 — students request
wash-and-fold and local laundromats handle it (think "Uber, but for laundry").

We're building a small ambassador team, and RAs/RDs are a natural fit: you already know your residents and
help them solve everyday problems, and laundry is a real one. Ambassadors earn commission for getting
students onto the app **and** for signing up local laundromats. It's flexible around your schedule, and it's
solid startup growth experience to put on a résumé.

As an ambassador you earn **30% of UniHamper's commission on every order from your campus**; I'll send the exact payout breakdown if you're curious.

Would you be open to a quick chat, or should I send the signup and a starter kit so you can look on your own time?

Thanks,
[Your name] · UniHamper · https://unihamper.com · @unihamperhq

*(Note: if pitching residence-life staff, confirm any school policy on students promoting outside services in
their RA/RD capacity. Keep it honest and above-board.)*

## 3. Ambassador Starter Kit — outline

Hand this to every confirmed ambassador so they never start from a blank page. Deliver as a shared doc/PDF.

### A. Welcome + how you get paid
- One-paragraph "what UniHamper is" (from source-of-truth).
- **How commission works:** you earn **30% of UniHamper's commission on every completed/delivered order from your campus**. **⚠️ CONFIRM** payout method & cadence (and whether a hard per-order $ example is OK to show).
- The two things you're recruiting: **students** (users) and **laundromats** (providers).
- Your links: **⚠️ CONFIRM: ambassador signup/referral URL**, IG @unihamperhq, TikTok @unihamper.

### B. Your pitch to students (copy/paste)
- **One-liner:** "Never do laundry again — students near campus request wash-and-fold and a local shop handles
  it. $0 until your wash starts, $9.99 flat pickup & delivery."
- **DM template:** "Ok this is kind of a game-changer for laundry — you request a pickup, a local shop washes &
  folds it, and it comes back done. Launching here Aug 1. Want the link?"
- **In-person one-liner** for tabling / dorm floors.
- Key honest facts they can repeat: $0 up front (hold, not a charge), $9.99 flat pickup/delivery, prices set by
  the local pro, live tracking, chat/call your provider. **Never quote a specific $/lb.**

### C. Your pitch to laundromats (copy/paste)
- **One-liner:** "New campus laundry marketplace — you keep 100% of your rate, we take $0 commission, you set
  your own prices, and it's free to list. Optional $9.99/run delivery income too."
- **Short DM/email** they can send an owner (link to the laundromat playbook's cold email for the full version).
- **What to collect:** owner's best email/phone + whether they do delivery, then send the "List your laundromat"
  signup. See [`laundromat-acquisition-playbook.md`](laundromat-acquisition-playbook.md) for full scripts + objection handling.

### D. Sample reposts (ready to share)
- 2–3 pre-written captions they can post to their story/feed (pull from
  [`../02-social/posts/supply-side-recruitment-posts.md`](../02-social/posts/supply-side-recruitment-posts.md)).
- A "share this to your story" graphic/template (from `03-visuals/` — brand kit is confirmed; see [`../03-visuals/brand-kit.md`](../03-visuals/brand-kit.md)).
- Tag rules: always tag @unihamperhq (IG) / @unihamper (TikTok) so we can repost and credit them.

### E. Do's & don'ts (keep us trustworthy)
- **Do:** be honest, use only confirmed facts, disclose you're an ambassador, be low-pressure.
- **Don't:** promise order volumes or earnings, invent prices, spam, or claim we set laundromat prices (we don't).
