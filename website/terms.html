5. The Bikes & New York City Rules

  • Certified equipment — Foodyzz e-bikes are certified to UL 2849 and their batteries to UL 2271 by TÜV Rheinland, an accredited testing laboratory, as New York City requires for a powered bicycle that is sold, leased, or rented. The certifying laboratory and the certificate numbers are shown on each bike's listing in the app, where you can also check them against the laboratory's public register. We keep proof of certification on file.
  • Battery charging is your responsibility — Foodyzz does not operate battery charging stations, swap stations, or battery storage for customers. While the bike is in your care you charge it yourself, and you must: use only the charger we supply, charge indoors in a ventilated space away from exits and anything flammable, never charge unattended overnight, never use a damaged or modified battery or charger, and never leave a battery charging after it is full.
  • Battery safety information — New York City and the Fire Department publish battery safety information, linked from every bike listing in the app. Stop using any battery that is hot, swollen, leaking, damaged, or behaving oddly, move it away from anything flammable, and tell us immediately. If a battery is smoking or on fire, call 911 — do not attempt to move or extinguish it yourself.
  • 15 mph speed limit — since October 24, 2025 New York City limits e-bikes to 15 mph on city streets and in bike lanes. Our bikes are configured to that limit. Do not modify, defeat, or attempt to raise the speed limiter; doing so is a serious breach of these Terms and voids the Protection Plan.
  • Riding as a commercial cyclist — New York City imposes additional rules on people who ride a bicycle for commercial purposes, including helmet, identification, and equipment requirements. Those rules apply to you and to the business you deliver for. Foodyzz is not your employer and does not assume them for you.
